[i]This plugin tilts you by 23.44 degrees.[/i]

Gives planets, dwarf planets and moons their real axial tilt, gives moons their real orbital inclination, and puts their parking orbits into each body's new equatorial plane.
[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Axial tilt changes the appearance of planets, not the game's climate model.
[*]The visual solstice drifts slightly over time because the orbital year differs from the calendar year. Loading a save recalibrates it.
[*][Orbit] points and parked ships follow the tilted equatorial planes; together with the moon orbital inclinations this changes the delta-v of within-system transfers.
[*]Asteroid parking orbits are polar around some planets in vanilla and remain so.
[*]Moon orbital inclinations and nodes use data for 2020-01-01. Their changes over time are not modeled.
[*]Magnetic axes, auroras and magnetospheres use the observed magnetic-axis tilt for Earth, Jupiter, Uranus, Neptune and Ganymede — bodies with intrinsic magnetic fields. Set “TiltMagneticAxes = false” to disable. (Saturn and Mercury also have intrinsic magnetic fields, but their magnetic axes nearly coincide with their rotation axes.)
[/list]
[h2]Magnetospheres and performance[/h2]
Dynamic magnetospheres are computationally demanding. By default, “Renderer = Auto” uses the GPU, with a CPU fallback if GPU rendering is unavailable. In that case “BepInEx\LogOutput.log” contains a warning that names the reason. You can also select CPU rendering manually with “Renderer = CPU”.
If performance suffers while magnetospheres are visible, try “DynamicFields = false”. This uses the vanilla shape, rotating it in the plane perpendicular to the Sun's direction instead of dynamically deforming it. Restart the game after changing settings.
[h2]Configuration[/h2]
Settings are in “BepInEx\config\com.lazyranma.tiltedworlds.cfg”, created after the first run.
